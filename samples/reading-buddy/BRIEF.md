# Reading Buddy — Brief Card

**Icon:** 📚
**One line:** Reading list, notes, and the book you meant to finish.
**What it does:** Keeps your to-read pile honest: what you are reading, what you finished, and the notes worth keeping.
**Parent:** Coordinator.
**Status:** brief card — during setup, your Muse fleshes this card out with
you (persona, first task, homepage) and it becomes a working buddy.
